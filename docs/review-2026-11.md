# Review and gameplan, November 2026

An outside read of Vektor Desk at 1.0.9 (`6dd10e3`): interface and menus, complexity, and the simulations and strategies section, followed by the rework plan. Personal figures stay out of the repo; the account analysis and projections sit in the project files as `research/stock-activity-and-projections-2026-11-10.md`.

## Verdict

| Area | Read | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lab honesty design | Strong | Rules not tickers, walk-forward, 2-year holdout, post-cutoff window, deflated Sharpe, overfit odds, factor check, survivorship stress, forward-only lane for anything a model read |
| Paper gate | Weak | 182 days of "excess ≥ 0" is close to a coin flip, and the status is recomputed every night |
| Leaderboard | Misleading | Every row is labelled against one benchmark and ranked on raw excess across different benchmarks and ages |
| Probability bands | Honest but uninformative | With alpha dropped, the mixes differ only by beta, yet the page crowns a "best odds of FI" |
| Catalog | Thin | Four static allocations; none tests the things that actually move a small concentrated account |
| Interface | Good parts, dated map | Clean components, 96 screens with no overflow; the menu still follows the single-plan app it started as |
| Complexity | Heavy | 10 model desks, about 24 scheduled jobs and about 50K lines for one small account |

## Simulations and strategies

### What holds up

| Piece | Why it is right |
|---|---|
| Strategies are written rules run by one simulator | No hand-picked trades, no leakage through a prompt |
| Walk-forward plus a 2-year holdout plus a post-cutoff window | Three separate chances for an overfit rule to fail |
| Bars set before the run (`runner.py`: deflated Sharpe 0.95, overfit odds 0.2, factor check, holdout at least half of walk-forward) | The verdict cannot be argued with afterwards |
| Forward-only lane for anything a model reads | The only honest way to test a model-read signal |
| Alpha dropped in the bands | The projection does not extrapolate a lucky decade |

### Rigor gaps

| # | Gap | Where | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1 | The 182-day gate passes on the sign of cumulative excess. A strategy with zero edge passes half the time; one that loses 5 points a year still passes 33 to 44% of the time (table below) | `lab/paper.py:45`, `lane.py:309`, `catalog.py:239` | "Eligible" says little about edge |
| F2 | Status is recomputed nightly from scratch, so a strategy can flip between eligible and retired day to day, and a catalog mix is flattened the day it dips under its benchmark | `catalog.py:234-240` | Churn in the paper account; a verdict that is not a verdict |
| F3 | The multiple-testing count for deflated Sharpe follows `parent_id`. The nightly desk mints a new id each night with no parent, so each night's 24 trials are counted alone | `runner.py:131`, `agents/strategy.py:62` | The bar gets easier the more nights the desk searches |
| F4 | "As held" is modelled and papered as rebalanced back to today's weights every period. A real hold drifts | `bands.py` `mix_history`, `catalog.py` `targets` | Overstates both the risk and the upside of simply holding |
| F5 | The leaderboard prints one benchmark for every row and sorts on raw excess, though rows carry different benchmarks and ages | `ui/src/lab/Leaderboard.jsx:72`, `leaderboard.py:181` | A 20-day row can outrank a 150-day row; mixes are shown against the wrong index |
| F6 | The bands rank mixes by median outcome, which is a beta ranking once alpha is dropped. Fees are charged to two mixes only. No lower-premium case, no tax or margin cost | `bands.py:362`, `ui/src/pages/Mixes.jsx:149` | "Best odds of FI" reads as advice and is noise |
| F7 | Lanes and families score only the trades that fit the slots, not every signal | `lane.py`, `lab/sim.py` | Few events, wide error bars, slow answers |
| F8 | A fixture shows overfit odds of 62% with the bar marked clear and the verdict "pass" (the bar is 20%) | `ui/src/fixtures/lab.strategies.sector-momentum.json` | Screens teach the wrong reading |

Gate power, simulated: chance that cumulative excess is at or above zero on day 182.

| True yearly edge | Tracking error 8% | 15% | 25% |
|---|---|---|---|
| −5 pts | 33% | 41% | 44% |
| 0 | 50% | 50% | 50% |
| +3 pts | 60% | 56% | 53% |
| +5 pts | 67% | 59% | 56% |
| +10 pts | 81% | 68% | 61% |

At 15% tracking error a real 5-point edge needs about 36 years to reach t = 2; a 10-point edge needs 9. Half a year of paper proves the plumbing works. It cannot prove the edge, and the app should say so.

### Creativity gaps

| # | Gap |
|---|---|
| 1 | The catalog is four fixed allocations. Three of them (S&P-like safe mix, tech tilt, dip-buy) produce nearly the same bands: median real return over 10 years of 7.2 to 8.3% a year and a 12 to 13% chance of a 10-year loss |
| 2 | Dip-buy holds cash waiting for a drop. That is a known drag in a rising market; it ships without the evidence for or against |
| 3 | Nothing tests position size, exits, margin or holding period, which are the levers a small stock-picking account actually has |
| 4 | Rule tests on habits exist (Patterns) but stop at description. None replays the account under a different rule |
| 5 | There is no null. A lane's result is never shown next to what random names from the same universe on the same dates would have done |

### New tests worth building

| # | Test | Question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| N1 | Replay with a per-buy size cap (5, 10, 15, 20% of the account), remainder into the index | Was the result selection or size? |
| N2 | Replay with an exit rule on single stocks (fixed stop at 15 to 30%, or close under the 200-day average) | Does cutting losers help after the winners it also cuts? |
| N3 | Replay with no margin, and with a 366-day minimum hold | What did leverage and short-term tax cost? |
| N4 | Hold versus rebalance versus trim-to-band for a conviction position | What the three choices look like over 1 to 20 years |
| N5 | Event study for every lane and family: every signal, fixed horizons, against a placebo of random names on the same dates | Is there a signal at all, in weeks instead of years? |
| N6 | Time to FI against monthly deposit, with a lower-premium case | How much the savings rate matters next to the mix |
| N7 | Core plus capped satellite, and a trend-filtered mix | Mixes that differ by something other than beta |

## Gameplan

Sizes: S is under a day of thread time, M one to two, L more.

### P0: numbers the app shows wrong today

| # | Action | Owner | Size |
|---|---|---|---|
| F1+F2 | Gate becomes a stored verdict at day 182 and at fixed checkpoints after. Pass needs the backtest bars, a minimum event count, and excess above a noise band; retire needs excess below the band at a checkpoint. No nightly flips, no flattening on a one-day dip. The row shows "chance this is luck" | Statistics | M |
| F3 | Deflated Sharpe counts every full-run trial in the family key (`insider-`, `drift-`, `filings-`) across ids and nights; the nightly path sets `parent_id` | Statistics, Agents | S |
| F4 | "As held" modelled and papered as a drifting hold, deposits going to band targets; rebalanced version kept as a labelled variant | Statistics | M |
| F5 | Leaderboard labels each row with its own benchmark, groups by benchmark, ranks on excess per unit of tracking error, and greys rows under 60 days | Design, Statistics | S |

## How the projections were run

| Step | Method |
|---|---|
| Activity | Broker history replayed to daily positions and cash, reconciled to current holdings; FIFO lots; time-weighted and money-weighted return; shadow portfolios that receive the same dollars on the same dates |
| Replays | Same deposits and dates, one rule changed (size cap or exit), difference in end value |
| Bands | The desk's own `bands.fit` and `bands.simulate`: beta to the US market, alpha dropped, 12-month block bootstrap of monthly market history since 1926, 2,000 paths, today's dollars at 2.5% inflation |
| Sober case | Same, with the market premium cut by 2 points a year |
| Hold versus rebalance | Two sleeves simulated separately and never rebalanced, against the blended monthly-rebalanced model |
| Gate power | Daily excess drawn at the stated edge and tracking error, 182 days, share of paths at or above zero |
